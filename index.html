PAT or HIPPY?

Parents as Teachers (PAT)

For families from pregnancy until kindergarten. A parent educator visits your home with activities that help your child learn and grow, and checks on how your child is developing.

HIPPY

For families with children ages 2 to 5. A home visitor from your community brings books and activities each week so you can help your child get ready for school.

Programs are listed by the counties they serve, not by office location. A program's office may be in a nearby town, since home visitors travel to families.
